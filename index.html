A pattern is a clue, not a cause.

At a fictional weekend kiosk, days with more cold-drink sales also tend to have more cycle hires. Explore one possible explanation.

Compare the same sixteen days

Association alone does not establish causation. Warm weather is a plausible shared explanation for both counts. This small constructed example cannot prove that weather is the only cause.

Data table and optional correlation measure

Pearson's r describes linear association from -1 to 1. Zero r does not rule out every kind of relationship. The chart and the table use exactly the same sixteen synthetic records.
